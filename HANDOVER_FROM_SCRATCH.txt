- Mark as date table: turn the toggle On first
- 'Calendar' needs quotes inside DAX (already done in the file)
- Background image fit: Fill, transparency 0%
- New Card: no Reference labels, no Action; rename the field for the label
- Home main chart: Calendar[Date] with X-axis type Continuous (no scrollbar)
- Copying GDP page to Inflation: choose Don't sync
